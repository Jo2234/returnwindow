Juniper Lane Goods — Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you bought. If something isn't right, here is how returns work.

Apparel and footwear may be returned within 30 days of purchase with tags attached.
Electronics may be returned within 14 days if unopened.
Clearance items marked FINAL SALE are not eligible for return.
All other items may be returned within 21 days of the date of purchase.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.
